# Wallpaper sources

All seven wallpapers are AI-generated with Codex's image generation in a flat inked comic style, then upscaled to 3840x2160 with Upscayl's Digital Art 4x model. The black page is filled with one exact colour, `#0A0806`. Saved as lossless PNG.

The figure and creatures are original to this theme.
